★ 3DS LL ★

this is my pink 3dsLL ! i bought it january 2025, and it came pre-modded which was perfect. i have always wanted a 3ds, and the pink color matched the DSLite i've had for years.
it came pre-modded, which saved a lot of time and stress from doing it myself. since then, i have added a ton of games, themes, badges, and just customized it to fit what i wanted out of the console!
cool stuff
  • iMango, a mp3 player that works with the lid closed (as long as you're wearing headphones). supports custom album covers!
  • fourthtube, a youtube client
  • lots of badges. some are offical, some are custom
games i like
  • streetpass games!!
  • tomodachi life
  • rhythm heaven megamix
  • cooking mama 5
  • pokemon black
i plan on buying a clear case and some stickers to decorate. i really enjoy bringing this thing around. i don't get a lot of streetpasses at home, but there are a few people on my college campus who streetpass me now and again!
i have a shit ton of games on this thing, but admittedly i haven't played a lot of them. some i want to try are miitopia, happy home designer, kirby's epic yarn, and ocarina of time.
i also have some old DS gamecards i carry around, id like to pull those out and play some of them more often.
sometimes i wish i had spent an extra hundred on the new3ds, so i could natively play things like minecraft and issac, but maybe i'll keep it in mind for the future :)
